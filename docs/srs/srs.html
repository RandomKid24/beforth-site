7Delivery and acceptance

7.1How we deliver

BeForth works in six stages. Each ends with something the client can review.

#StageWhat the client receivesTarget date
1DiscoveryA written map of the current process, users and bottlenecks.[Date]
2ArchitectureDatabase, application structure, roles, permissions and integrations.[Date]
3DevelopmentWorking software in stages, reviewed throughout.[Date]
4Testing and onboardingValidated behaviour, migrated data and trained users.[Date]
5DeploymentRelease for real use, with the infrastructure it needs.[Date]
6OptimizationMonitoring, improvement and extension as requirements change.[Ongoing]

7.2Acceptance criteria

The system is accepted when all of the following are true.

  • Every Must requirement in section 3 passes its check.
  • The client's users have completed user acceptance testing, and open defects are agreed in writing.
  • The non-functional targets in section 5 are met or a signed exception is recorded.
  • The client sponsor and BeForth project lead sign the approvals page.

7.3Changing a requirement

Requirements can change. Any change is written down, reviewed for its effect on scope, schedule and cost, approved by both sides, and recorded in the revision history before work starts.
