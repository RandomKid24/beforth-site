## Reddit answer

*Use where someone asks how to choose between custom software and a packaged product. Disclose the affiliation in the first line.*

I work at BeForth, so take this with the appropriate pinch of salt — but the honest answer is that it depends on how unusual your process is.

If your workflows, roles and reporting fit a standard product closely, use the standard product. If you find yourself building spreadsheets around it to handle approvals, stock movements or the way your teams actually hand work over, that is when a custom system starts to make sense.

What we do is map the process first, then put the scope and cost in writing before anyone builds. You approve it first. We publish no price list and no fixed timeline, because both would be guesses before discovery.

If you want to see the shape of the systems we build, there are 13 working previews on labelled sample data with no signup: https://www.beforth.in/portfolio. The short write-up on how we arrive at a number is here: https://www.beforth.in/pricing. We reply within one working day.

## Quora answer

**Question:** How do I decide whether a business needs custom software or an off-the-shelf tool?

Start from the process, not the software. Write down the handful of workflows that currently depend on spreadsheets, chat messages or someone's memory. If a standard product handles them without workarounds, buy it — that is usually the cheaper and simpler answer.

Consider a custom system when those workflows are genuinely specific: approval rules that differ by role, stock or data that has to move between systems that do not normally talk, reports that have to be assembled by hand every month, or multiple locations that need one view. A custom system is worth discussing when the software can follow your process instead of the other way round.

Whoever you speak to, ask for the scope and cost in writing before any build starts, and ask what happens after launch — training, data migration, support and later changes. We publish our method here: https://www.beforth.in/pricing.
